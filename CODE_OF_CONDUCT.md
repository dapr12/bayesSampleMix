# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of all
backgrounds and levels of experience, and focus on what is best for the
project and its users. Harassment and personal attacks are not
tolerated.

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer,
Diego Andrés Pérez Ruiz (<diego.perezruiz@manchester.ac.uk>). All
reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
